Professional Background

Engineered Solutions at the Intersection of AI & Real-World Impact

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham with a strong foundation in machine learning, deep neural networks, computer vision, and data-driven field research.

My technical trajectory centers on developing computationally efficient surrogate models to replace expensive physical and numerical simulations. In my final year project research, I worked on a Data-Driven Multi-Output Deep Neural Network & Random Forest Framework predicting thermodynamic formation energy and total energy for Cobalt-Gold (CoAu) nanoparticles across 15,071 configurations using 1,084 structural and Generalized Coordination Number (GCN) descriptors.

Beyond predictive modeling, I excel in competitive hackathons and field action research. My team secured 2nd Place at the IIT Roorkee Road Safety Hackathon for EmergenSee—a Convolutional Neural Network system dispatching geo-tagged SMS alerts in 5–7 seconds. Additionally, I co-authored field research in Nongkya Village, Meghalaya (Live-in-Labs® Program), published on SSRN (Elsevier).
